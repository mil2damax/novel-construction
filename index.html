How we run a project

Full lifecycle, one team.

Estimating, procurement, subcontractor management, compliance, and project administration — held under a single point of contact from bid to closeout.

  1. 01

    Estimating & preconstruction

    Scope development, budgeting, and bid-level takeoffs before a shovel goes in the ground.

  2. 02

    Procurement & materials

    Sourcing and tracking materials across trades — from EIFS panels to electrical gear — against brand and code specs.

  3. 03

    Subcontractor management

    Scheduling and coordinating trade partners, holding every subcontract to its terms and schedule, staying accountable, and building strong, sustainable relationships.

  4. 04

    Compliance & brand standards

    Verifying every install — outlets, fixtures, finishes — against brand standards and IBC.

  5. 05

    Project administration

    Documentation, inspections, and closeout, so ownership groups have a clean record start to finish.

Why Novel

What ownership groups get from us.

Licensed & compliance-first

Novel Construction is a licensed North Carolina general contractor. Every new venture — from subcontractor agreements to trade licensing — is structured for compliance before it's structured for speed.
