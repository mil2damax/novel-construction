How we run a project

Full lifecycle, one team.

Estimating, procurement, subcontractor management, compliance, and project administration, all held under a single point of contact from bid to closeout.

  1. 01

    Estimating & preconstruction

    Scope development, budgeting, and bid-level takeoffs before a shovel goes in the ground.

  2. 02

    Procurement & materials

    Sourcing and tracking materials across trades, from EIFS panels to electrical gear, against brand and code specs.

  3. 03

    Subcontractor management

    Scheduling and coordinating trade partners, holding every subcontract to its terms and schedule, staying accountable, and building strong, sustainable relationships.

  4. 04

    Compliance & brand standards

    Verifying every install, from outlets and fixtures to finishes, against brand standards and IBC.

  5. 05

    Project administration

    Documentation, inspections, and closeout, so ownership groups have a clean record start to finish.

Our Approach

Care, ownership, and a methodical pursuit of quality.

That standard shapes every decision on a job site, including how materials are sourced. Every order is checked against spec before it's placed, and long-standing supplier relationships are used with purpose, so the outcome is never subpar materials or a subpar build.

01

Verified before it's ordered

Every quote is checked against spec and brand standards before a PO goes out, catching substitutions and quantity errors before they become field problems.

02

Relationships that lower cost

Established accounts across big-box and specialty distributors mean better pricing and priority access on the materials that matter.

03

One verified list, start to finish

A delivery checked against the order becomes the starting inventory for the job site, so there's no guessing what's actually on hand.

Why Novel

What ownership groups get from us.

01

Licensed & compliance-first

Novel Construction is a licensed North Carolina general contractor. Every new venture, from subcontractor agreements to trade licensing, is structured for compliance before it's structured for speed.

02

Brand standards, verified line by line

Fixtures, finishes, and rough-in are checked line by line against brand standards and the International Building Code.

03

Documentation-forward

Site conditions, subcontractor communications, and inspections are documented as they happen, protecting the schedule and the owner.

04

One point of contact, start to finish

Estimating, procurement, trade coordination, and closeout run under a single team, so ownership groups aren't managing the gaps between contractors.

05

Built with an owner's eye

Having served as Owner's Representative on past projects, that same perspective carries into every build, so owners and their managers have less to worry about.
