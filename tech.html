Site Materials Inventory

One app, every trade, every job site.

Materials on site are organized by trade and location. When a worker uses something, they clock it out, and the record — what was taken, by whom, from where, and when — is permanent.

  1. 01

    Verified deliveries become inventory

    When a delivery arrives and gets checked against the order, that verified list seeds the starting inventory for the job site — no re-typing a count from scratch.

  2. 02

    Tracked by trade and location

    Every material is tagged to a trade — electrical, plumbing, framing, and more — and a physical location on site, like a laydown yard or a specific storage box.

  3. 03

    Workers clock out what they use

    A worker picks their name, selects the materials and quantities they're taking, and confirms. Inventory updates immediately, no separate paperwork.

  4. 04

    A permanent record, every time

    Every clock-out and delivery is logged and filterable by trade, material, worker, or date — a running audit trail of the whole job site.

Capabilities

What it does today.

01

Real-time inventory

Stock levels update the moment a worker clocks material out, visible instantly from the office dashboard.

02

PIN-based field login

Workers pick their name and enter a 4-digit PIN, so confirming identity is fast enough that it actually gets used on site.

03

Full audit trail

Every clock-out and delivery is a permanent record — searchable by trade, material, worker, or date range.

04

Rough-in & trim aware

Materials that install in phases — like electrical, plumbing, and mechanical — are tracked by phase, since rough-in and trim often arrive in separate deliveries weeks apart.

In development

The first working build is complete and currently being reviewed before rollout to job sites. Offline support for low-signal areas is the next thing being built, so a clock-out still gets recorded even without a signal on site.
